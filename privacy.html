Crew — Privacy Policy

Effective Nov 25, 2026

Crew is a fitness-accountability app for small groups of friends, made by Accelitude. This policy describes exactly what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and, from your sign-in provider (Apple or Google), a verified identifier and email. We never see your password.
  • Health data: workout summaries only — type, start/end time, duration, active calories, and the recording source — read from Apple Health with your permission, solely to verify your crew commitments. We never read routes, GPS, heart rate, or any other health records. Hand-typed Health entries are ignored.
  • Content you create: crew chat messages, photos you post, your commitments, and settlement records (who owes whom within your crew).
  • Optional: a profile/crew photo and payment handles (Venmo/Zelle) you choose to add, shown only to your crew.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics SDKs, no sale or sharing of data with third parties.
  • Health data is never used for anything except scoring your own crew's week, and is never shared outside your crew.
  • Payments happen in your own Venmo/Zelle apps — Crew never holds money or payment credentials.

Where it lives

Data is stored on our server (hosted on Fly.io, in the United States), encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your crew's members can see your workout cards, week results, and chat; nobody else can.

Deleting your data

In the app: You → Delete account. Your sign-in is erased immediately, your profile is anonymized to “Departed member”, and your personal details (name, photo, handles) are removed. Aggregate crew history (week results and any open debts) remains with your crewmates, no longer tied to your identity.
